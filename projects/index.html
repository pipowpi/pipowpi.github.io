+−Why GNM

SMIRK estimates a 3D face from a single photo by predicting parameters for the FLAME head model. GNM is a newer head model whose 383 expression controls are grouped by face region and include teeth and tongue. The goal is to swap it in, fine-tune on the FEED dataset, and compare the result against the original SMIRK.

Render + rebuild

The rebuilt face is compared with the input

Comparing the rebuilt face with the photo is the training signal, so no 3D labels are needed.

Done

  • Done: Ran the pretrained SMIRK baseline and inspected its parameters, mesh and reconstruction.
  • Done: Unblocked FLAME loading by converting its Chumpy-dependent model file to NumPy data.
  • Done: Traced the encoders, renderer, image translator and training losses.
  • Done: Mapped GNM’s interface: 253 identity and 383 expression coefficients, and 17,821 vertices against FLAME’s 5,023.

Next

  • Next: Connect GNM to the encoders and renderer, with new landmark and face-mask mappings.
  • Next: Revise FLAME-specific losses and confirm training works on a small subset.
  • Next: Fine-tune on FEED and evaluate against SMIRK on held-out images.
