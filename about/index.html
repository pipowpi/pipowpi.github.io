How I work

The habits behind the work

  • Write it down to understand it

    If I cannot explain a system in plain words, I do not understand it yet. Design notes and the posts on this site come from that habit.

  • Give the risky part the fewest doors

    On the GPU CI work, runners pulled jobs so nothing outside the cluster needed a way in. I start security from the data flow, not the checklist.

  • Measure before fixing

    The 10-minute telemetry dashboards were a query problem. Finding the bottleneck first turned the fix into a change in how queries ran, and load times dropped to under a second.

  • Teach what I learn

    At Abzooba I ran regular DevOps and MLOps sessions and mentored 4 engineers. Explaining things to others is how I find the gaps in my own understanding.
